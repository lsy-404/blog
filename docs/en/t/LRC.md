## What Is It

V Universe Lyrics Hub is a site dedicated to collecting and sharing lyrics for V-family (virtual singer) albums, with version control and community collaboration maintained through a GitHub repository.

## Features

- **Album navigation**: Organized by album, with support for quick search
- **Multiple download options**: Download the entire library, a single album package, or a single song
- **LRC format**: A standard lyrics format compatible with mainstream players and lyrics plugins
- **Ongoing additions**: Dozens of albums are currently included, covering V-family singers such as Yanhe and Luo Tianyi
