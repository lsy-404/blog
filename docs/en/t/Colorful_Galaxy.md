Stellaris NSFW Mod "Colorful Galaxy": Installation and How to Open the Menu

<!-- more -->

*This tutorial only applies to the Steam version of Stellaris. Due to the publication date, some content may not match the current situation.

## Installation

Press Win+R, enter `steam://open/console`, and press Enter to open the Steam console. Enter `workshop_download_item 281990 2923834739`, press Enter, and wait for the resource pack *Selflessness Was Never You, Following Him Without Understanding Her* to finish downloading. Then enter `workshop_download_item 281990 2748029219`, press Enter, and wait for the script pack *Colorful Galaxy* to finish downloading. Once the downloads are complete, close the console.

Launch the game and make sure the mods are enabled in your playset, with the resource pack placed before the script pack.

Start the game, enjoy it!

## Opening the NSFW Menu

Go to Stellaris - Government - Edicts, and you will see a one-day edict that only costs 5 gems to activate.

*Note: look carefully at the Star Sea Commonwealth edict and don't click it by mistake, or it will directly start a war.
